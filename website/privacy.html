Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us at Verteon.

Data Collection

Verteon collects minimal data necessary to provide AI coding assistance services. This includes:

  • Account information (name, email address)
  • Project metadata for AI assistance context
  • Usage statistics to improve the service

Data Usage

Your data is used to:

  • Provide AI coding assistance
  • Maintain project context across sessions
  • Improve AI model performance
  • Send important service updates

Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits
  • Minimal data retention policies

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request data deletion
  • Object to data processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Contact us at privacy@verteon.ai for any privacy-related requests.
